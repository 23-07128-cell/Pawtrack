ABOUT PAWTRACK

A Community That Cares

PAWTRACK is a community-based stray animal monitoring and reporting system designed to help residents share information about animals that may need attention.

Why PAWTRACK Matters

Stray animals can be overlooked when residents do not know where to report them or whom to contact. PAWTRACK provides a central place to submit reports and find helpful resources.

By encouraging responsible reporting and community participation, the system aims to help residents, barangay officials, and animal welfare partners coordinate their efforts.

PAWTRACK TIPS

Learn How You Can Help

Use the arrows to explore practical animal welfare tips.

📍

Remember the Location

Use a clear landmark or street name when reporting a stray animal. Accurate details can make follow-up easier.

🐾

Observe from a Safe Distance

Avoid chasing or cornering unfamiliar animals. Give them space and seek professional help if they appear injured or distressed.

📷

Describe What You See

If it is safe, note the animal's appearance, number, condition, and when it was seen. Never put yourself at risk to take a photo.

🤝

Contact the Right People

Reach out to your barangay, a veterinary clinic, or an animal welfare organization for guidance on appropriate next steps.
