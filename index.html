About us

A safer, kinder community for stray animals.

PAWTRACK is a community-based idea that helps residents share information about stray animals that may need attention. It aims to connect reports with local people and organizations that can provide guidance or assistance.

By making reports easier to organize, the community can become more aware of animals that may be injured, sick, lost, or in unsafe surroundings.
