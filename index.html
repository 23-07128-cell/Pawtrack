About PAWTRACK

PAWTRACK is a community-based stray animal monitoring and reporting system for residential communities. It helps organize reports and supports coordination with barangay officials and animal welfare partners.

Animal Welfare Resources

Simple reminders for residents who encounter stray animals.

Keep a Safe Distance

Approach unfamiliar animals carefully. Do not force contact with an animal that appears frightened or aggressive.

Observe and Report

Note the location and visible condition. Avoid putting yourself or the animal in danger while taking a photo.

Seek Appropriate Help

Contact local animal welfare services or a veterinary professional when an animal appears injured or seriously ill.
